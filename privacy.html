Privacy policy

The short version: we collect only what you give us, we use it only to reply to you and run your project, and we never sell it. Here’s the full version.

What we collect

When you use our contact form or email us, we receive the details you choose to share: your name, business name, email address, phone number, and anything you write in your message. Our hosting provider also keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) to keep the site secure and fast.

How we use it

We use your contact details to reply to your enquiry, prepare your quote, and — if you become a client — deliver and support your project. That’s it. We don’t sell your information, rent it, or share it with third parties for their marketing.

Who processes it

Form submissions are delivered by our form provider, and the site is hosted on Vercel. Both act as processors on our behalf and store data under their own security practices.

Analytics & advertising

If you reached us through an advert, we may use Google Analytics and advertising measurement tools from Google or Meta to count visits and record that an enquiry was submitted. These set cookies and record your approximate location and the pages you view. We use them only to understand which adverts bring genuine enquiries — we do not use them to build profiles about you, and we never sell your data.

You can opt out with Google’s browser add-on, through your browser’s cookie settings, or with any tracker-blocking extension. Blocking them does not affect your ability to use this site or contact us.

How long we keep it

Enquiry emails are kept while we might still work together, and client records for as long as needed to support your site and meet legal obligations. Ask us to delete your details at any time and we will, unless a legal requirement says otherwise.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it — just email team@simplicitybuilds.com and we’ll sort it out within a few business days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will be called out plainly, not buried.
