## 1. Name options

The site is built with **"Simplicity Builds"** as a placeholder. Here's a shortlist —
each reads premium and works for a web-design studio. Check the `.com` before
you commit (see below), then rename with the one-liner in the README.

| Name | Vibe | Domain ideas to check |
|------|------|-----------------------|
| **Simplicity Builds** | Premium, guiding, distinctive (current build) | simplicitybuilds.com · northlightweb.com · getnorthlight.com |
| **Meridian** | High-end agency, "peak / point on the map" | meridianweb.co · meridian.studio · trymeridian.com |
| **Vela** | Short, modern, constellation ("the sails") | vela.studio · velaweb.co · vela.build |
| **Beacon** | "Get seen / get found" — great for local SEO angle | beacon.studio · beaconsites.com · getbeacon.co |
| **Foundry** | Craft, "we forge sites" | webfoundry.co · foundrystudio.co · foundry.build |
| **Lumen / Lumo** | Light, clean, techy | lumowebstudio.com · getlumo.com · lumo.design |
| **Apex** | Bold, top-tier | apexwebstudio.com · apex.site · goapex.co |

**Tips for picking:**
- `.com` is still king for trust. If it's taken, `get<name>.com` or
  `<name>.studio` / `.co` / `.design` are all fine for a studio.
- Say it out loud — it should be easy to spell over the phone.
- Check the name isn't a big existing company in tech (Lumen is a telecom, for
  example) to avoid confusion.
- Grab the matching Instagram / Facebook handle at the same time.

> **Recommendation:** *Simplicity Builds* or *Beacon* if you want to lean on the
> "get your business seen" story; *Meridian* or *Vela* if you want to feel more
> like a premium boutique studio.

## 2. Pricing strategy

### The market (why $49.99 wins)

| Competitor type | Real price (verified Aug 2026) | What they deliver | Your opening |
|---|---|---|---|
| "$97/mo" marketing bundlers (Townsquare, Hibu, Scorpion, ProSites) | Quoted ~$97 — **actually billed $300–$1,500+/mo** bundled, 12–24 mo contracts | Template/proprietary-platform site, ticket support, **site held hostage on cancel** | Massively overpriced & locked-in; our prices are published |
| Flat-rate $99/mo done-for-you shops (CalTech Web, Worldwide Web Designs, Jottful) | $99/mo, $0 setup, month-to-month, unlimited updates | Custom site + hosting + updates; some offer money-back guarantees | **Closest real competitors.** We win on annual price ($58.33 Grow annual, $69.99 monthly) + e-commerce/booking at Scale; watch our setup fees & edit caps vs their $0/unlimited |
| Freelancers | $500–5,000 one-time (professional work starts ~$1,500–2,500) | Custom build, then you're alone | Big upfront, no support |
| Agencies | $3,000–$30,000+ one-time; small-biz median ~$5,000 | Custom build + retainer upsell ($35–100/mo maintenance) | Own-It at $799 + $15/mo hosting is near-unique below $1,000 |
| DIY builders (Wix/Squarespace/GoDaddy) | Advertised from $3 promo — realistically $25–54/mo for a commerce-capable tier | You do all the work | Time + skill gap; nobody makes the next change for them |
| AI builders (Durable, 10Web, etc.) | $10–49/mo | AI-generated site, zero human service | Cheap but unmaintained; "who makes your next change?" |
| Cheap gig builds (Fiverr) | $50–750 one-time | Fast, quality/rework risk | No maintenance |

You undercut what the "$97 bracket" actually bills (typically $300+/mo), and you match or beat the honest $99 flat-raters on annual billing. Your true cost per client is only a few
dollars a month, so $49.99 is still ~90% gross margin. You're not cutting into
delivery cost — you're cutting the **fat** (ad budgets + lock-in margin) the
big players bake in.

### The package ladder (what's on the site)

**Model: monthly-first + a setup fee waived on annual prepay.** Not pure
monthly — the setup fee protects your build labor if a client churns in month
two. Not pure one-time — recurring revenue is the whole point, and it matches
the "$97/mo" framing buyers shop against.

| Tier | Price | Build | Included monthly |
|------|-------|-------|------------------|
| **Launch** | $49.99/mo + $99 setup* | Up to 5 pages, custom design, contact form, SSL | Hosting, uptime, **2 edits/mo** |
| **Grow** ⭐ | $69.99/mo + $149 setup* | Up to 12 pages, blog/CMS, on-page SEO, lead forms | + **5 edits/mo**, backups, Google Business |
| **Scale** | $149.99/mo + $299 setup* | Up to 30 pages OR store/booking, custom design | + **priority 24h edits**, analytics report |
| **Own-It Build** | $799 one-time + $15/mo | Full build, you own it | Hosting, SSL, uptime only |

\* *Setup fee waived on annual prepay; annual = 2 months free.*

**Note:** your *middle* tier ($69.99) now undercuts even the honest $99 flat-raters — while the "$97 ads" crowd really bills $300+.

### 6 ways you're **better** than the $97 ads
1. **You own it** — domain, site, content. Full handoff on request.
2. **No lock-in** — month-to-month; annual is a discount, not a trap.
3. **A real human** — not an offshore ticket queue.
4. **Speed** — live in ~7 days, not weeks.
5. **Transparent pricing** — it's all on the site; no "call for a quote."
6. **Cheaper for the same-or-better** — ~48% under $97, middle tier still under it.

### Risks to respect
1. **Never offer "unlimited edits."** The edit caps are your most important
   line item. Overflow → $29 / 30 min.
2. **Churn eats build labor** — that's what the setup fee / annual commit is for.
   Don't waive it on casual month-to-month deals.
3. **Undercharging trap** — $49.99 attracts the most price-sensitive buyers.
   Always value-anchor against the $97 so "cheap" reads as *savings*, not *low quality*.
4. **Solo ceiling ≈ 40–70 clients.** Before you hit it: raise prices,
   systematize (reusable templates, a self-serve edit request form), or add help.
5. **Scope creep** — publish a clear "not included" list; every custom ask maps
   to an add-on.
6. **Low-ticket payment friction** — turn on Stripe retries/dunning from day one.
