Our story

Great websites shouldn’t be a rich-company thing.

Northlight exists to give small businesses the kind of website big brands pay thousands for — at a price that makes sense, without the contracts and games.

We kept seeing the same thing: hardworking local businesses paying $97, $150, even $300 a month for a cookie-cutter template they didn’t even own. Miss a payment or try to leave? The whole site disappears.

That never sat right with us. So we built the opposite. Northlight hand-crafts premium websites, hosts them on fast modern infrastructure, and maintains them for a fair flat fee — starting at $49.99/month. You own your site and your domain. You can leave whenever you want. You won’t want to.

We keep our costs lean and our process tight, and we pass the savings straight to you. No bloated ad budgets to recover, no offshore ticket queues, no surprise invoices. Just a genuinely good website and a real person who picks up when you need something changed.
